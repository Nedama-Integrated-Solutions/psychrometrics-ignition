## Murphy and Koop (2005)

Saturation vapour pressure over supercooled water below 0 °C uses the equation published in
D. M. Murphy and T. Koop, "Review of the vapour pressures of ice and supercooled water for
atmospheric applications", Quarterly Journal of the Royal Meteorological Society 131 (2005),
pages 1539 to 1565.
